## 🚨 The Problem

Sepsis is one of the leading causes of preventable ICU mortality worldwide. Yet the systems meant to catch it are fundamentally broken:

| Stat | Reality |
|------|---------|
| 🔴 1 in 3 ICU deaths | Linked to sepsis |
| ⏱️ ~8% mortality rise | For every hour of delayed treatment |
| 📢 80%+ false alarms | Generated by traditional SIRS-based systems |

Traditional early-warning systems lack personalization, real-time adaptability, and explainability — leading to **alert fatigue** that causes clinicians to ignore the very warnings designed to save lives.

---

## 💡 What is VitalSense AI?

**VitalSense AI** is a real-time sepsis prediction engine that integrates directly with Electronic Medical Records (EMR) to deliver **explainable, early-warning alerts up to 6 hours before clinical onset** — giving care teams the intervention lead-time that saves lives.

```
Traditional Systems → React after symptoms appear → Too late
VitalSense AI       → Predict 6 hours before onset → Proactive care
```

## 🧠 AI/ML Architecture

### Dual-Model Prediction Core

**LSTM — Temporal Sequential Modeling**
- Captures deterioration patterns across time-series vitals
- Multi-hour look-back window for sequential trend analysis
- Detects subtle physiological shifts before clinical thresholds are crossed

**XGBoost — Structured Risk Classification**
- High precision classification on tabular EMR data
- Robust handling of missing/sparse ICU data
- Complementary to LSTM for structured feature signals

### Explainability Layer — SHAP
- Generates per-alert feature importance scores
- Clinicians see *exactly* which vitals or lab values triggered each alert
- Directly combats the trust gap causing alert fatigue in conventional systems

**Training Data:** 40,000+ real ICU patient records (MIMIC-IV dataset)

## 🔬 Innovation Highlights

- **6-Hour Predictive Lead-Time** — Predicts sepsis onset before standard clinical thresholds, enabling proactive (not reactive) care
- **Hybrid LSTM + XGBoost** — Dual-model design captures both sequential temporal and structured tabular clinical signals simultaneously
- **SHAP-Powered Explainability** — Every alert includes human-readable clinical rationale, not just a risk score
- **EMR-Native Integration** — Direct connection to hospital data streams; zero friction, zero extra data entry for clinical staff
- **Continuous Retraining Pipeline** — Designed for hospital-specific data drift adaptation over time
